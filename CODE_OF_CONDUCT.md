# Code of conduct

Ghostbuster is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and make room for people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
doxxing, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms the
community. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and public spaces
where someone is representing the project.
